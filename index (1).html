FireSync: one map for every fire hotspot

FireSync merges active-fire detections from two satellite sensors, MODIS (1 km) and VIIRS (375 m), into a single harmonized layer. Compare the sensors, track fire spread over time, and find the regions most prone to burning. Demo data is simulated, with fire regions placed around the world.

How the unification works

  1. Ingest: pull MODIS and VIIRS active-fire data (for example from NASA FIRMS).
  2. Normalize: one schema, UTC time, confidence mapped to a common 0 to 100 scale.
  3. Match: link detections within about 1 km and a few hours.
  4. Merge: output one hotspot with sensors, confidence and FRP.
  5. Analyze: count hotspots on a grid to find fire-prone areas and trends.

What you can do with it

  • Compare satellite results side by side.
  • Locate fires more precisely by combining both sensors.
  • Follow how a fire spreads day by day.
  • Rank the areas most exposed to fires.

Planned features

  • Live feed from the NASA FIRMS API.
  • Date range and confidence filters.
  • Export unified hotspots as CSV or GeoJSON.
  • Alerts for new fires near a chosen area.
